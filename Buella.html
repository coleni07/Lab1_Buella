About

Marginalia is written by someone who reads too many footnotes and takes too many notes in the margins of other people's books. It's a small, slow publication about attention, writing, and the ordinary work of thinking clearly.

New essays go up roughly every two weeks. No spam, no scheduling gimmicks — just when there's something worth saying.
